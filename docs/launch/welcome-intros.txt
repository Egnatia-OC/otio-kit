WELCOME / INTRODUCTION THREAD

Two lengths. Use the short one in Discord #introductions and similar; the long one
where an intro thread expects a paragraph. Neither opens with the project name: an
introduction that starts with a product reads as an advert, and people stop reading
at the first word they recognise as marketing.

Rules applied here: no link unless the thread says links are welcome; no bullets; one
concrete thing you did; one question or offer; your name if the community uses real
names.

---

## Long (forum, ~140 words)

Hi, I'm [name]. I edit and I write Python, and the Python mostly runs outside Resolve.

When 21.1 moved scripting to Studio I wanted to know exactly what was left on the
free build, rather than reading the release notes and guessing. So I tested it: the
Scripts menu loads nothing, the external socket refuses connections, and the console
that remains is Lua-only and sandboxed. One machine, one build, method written down,
and it is all public because "works for me" is not useful to anyone else.

The thing that survived is file import, so I have been building around that: a small
compiler that turns a written brief into an OTIO timeline you import like any other
file. It runs on Linux and Windows, macOS is untested, and I say so.

Happy to share the test notes if they are useful here, and curious what everyone
else is doing now that the old routes are gone.

## What not to do

- Do not paste the same text into three servers in one evening. Write a fresh first
  line each time; the rest can repeat.
- Do not lead with "I built X". Lead with what you measured.
- Do not ask for stars, tests, or feedback on the first message. Ask whether the
  measurements are useful; that is a question people can answer.
- Do not mention AI or LLMs in the intro. In these communities it invites a
  different conversation than the one you want.
